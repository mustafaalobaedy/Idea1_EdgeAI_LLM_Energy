# Annotated, verified reference library

Paper (working title): *Joules per Correct Answer: Energy-Accuracy-Latency Trade-offs of Quantized Small Language Models across Power Modes on an Edge GPU (NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin)*

Compiled 2026-10-03. Companion files: `references.bib` (BibTeX) and `references.ris` (RIS), with the same 98 entries and the same citation keys.

**Verification policy.** Every entry was checked against at least one authoritative source:

- the CrossRef REST API record for its DOI (authors, title, venue, year, volume, issue, pages);
- the arXiv API (`export.arxiv.org`);
- the official proceedings page (NeurIPS, PMLR, MLSys, JMLR, USENIX, ICLR virtual site), read through its `citation_*` metadata;
- the OpenReview API (for ICLR and TMLR venue status);
- or the official vendor or repository URL (fetched successfully with HTTP 200).

Summaries state only what the abstract, or the official page for software and docs, says. Where a peer-reviewed version exists, it is cited instead of the preprint, and the arXiv ID is kept for convenience.

Entries marked **[CLOSEST]** are the closest related work. They are discussed in the section 'Closest related work and how our study differs' below.

## Closest related work and how our study differs

Our study's distinguishing combination is as follows.

- **(a) Power modes:** an nvpmodel sweep (15W/30W/40W/MAXN) on one Jetson AGX Orin.
- **(b) Metric:** on-module INA3221 energy normalized per correct answer (J/correct), alongside latency.
- **(c) Quantization ladder:** a full GGUF ladder (Q3_K_M to Q8_0 and FP16) served via Ollama/llama.cpp.
- **(d) Model families:** four modern SLM families from 0.5B to 8B (Qwen2.5, Llama 3.2, Gemma 2, Qwen3).
- **(e) Thinking on/off:** reasoning switched on and off within the same Qwen3 model.
- **(f) Cascade:** a small-to-large cascade evaluated in energy terms.
- **(g) Benchmarks and statistics:** three accuracy benchmarks (GSM8K, ARC-Challenge, BoolQ) with paired significance testing.

## Notes for the authors

- **Power-mode naming.** The official NVIDIA Jetson Linux Developer Guide (r36.4) lists MaxN/15W/30W/**40W** for the **Jetson AGX Orin 32GB**, and MaxN/15W/30W/**50W** for the **AGX Orin 64GB**. Our 40W mode therefore implies the 32GB module (or a custom mode), so state the module variant explicitly in the paper. Cite `nvidia2024jetsonpower`.
- **GPTQ naming.** The ICLR 2023 version of GPTQ is titled *OPTQ: Accurate Quantization for Generative Pre-trained Transformers*. The key `frantar2023gptq` points to that version, with a note giving the GPTQ name and the arXiv ID.
- **Contamination reference.** `sainz2023nlp` is the CrossRef-registered Findings of EMNLP **2023** version (DOI 10.18653/v1/2023.findings-emnlp.722), even though the arXiv comment mentions EMNLP 2024 Findings.
- **Author lists.** Lists with more than 15 authors are truncated to the first 10 followed by 'and others' (BibTeX) or 'others' (RIS).
- **Accessed dates.** Software, documentation and vendor pages carry an 'Accessed 3 October 2026' note. Pin the exact llama.cpp/Ollama versions or commits used in the experiments in the paper's methods section.
